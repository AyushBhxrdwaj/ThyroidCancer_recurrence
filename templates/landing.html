About the Project

What is ThyroPredict?

ThyroPredict is an AI-driven tool designed to help assess the risk of thyroid cancer recurrence. By analyzing key patient clinical features such as age, gender, risk classification, and other medical parameters, our trained Random Forest model provides a probability-based prediction along with a detailed, human-readable explanation generated by Google's Gemini AI.

Clinically Informed

Built on real-world thyroid cancer datasets with carefully engineered features for maximum predictive power.

Explainable AI

Every prediction comes with a plain-language explanation — no confusing medical codes or jargon.

PDF Reports

Generate and download beautifully formatted clinical-style PDF reports for your records.
